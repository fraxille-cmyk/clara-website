Polyvinylpyrrolidone-vinyl acetate copolymer (E1208)

E1208AdditiveNeutral · limited concern

Diets

✓ Suits every diet Clara checks — as an ingredient

A synthetic polymer used as a glazing agent to coat food supplements, with a daily intake limit established by European regulators.

What it is

Polyvinylpyrrolidone-vinyl acetate copolymer is a synthetic substance made by combining two different chemical compounds into a single chain. It is a polymer, which means it is a large molecule made of repeating units, similar in structure to certain plastics. In the food industry, it is used exclusively as a glazing agent to create a protective, shiny coating on the surface of solid food supplements, helping them remain stable and easy to swallow.

How it's made

This ingredient is entirely synthetic and is produced in a controlled chemical manufacturing facility. It is made by the copolymerisation of two specific monomers, vinylpyrrolidone and vinyl acetate, in an organic solvent or in water. The reaction links these molecules together into a stable chain. Once the polymerisation is complete, the solvent is removed, and the resulting material is purified, dried, and ground into a fine powder suitable for use in food coatings.

Why manufacturers use it

Manufacturers use this polymer because it is an exceptionally strong film-forming agent. It creates a tough, flexible, and moisture-resistant barrier around tablets and capsules. This coating prevents the ingredients inside from absorbing moisture from the air, which can cause them to degrade. It also masks unpleasant tastes or odours from vitamins and minerals, and provides a smooth surface that makes large pills much easier for consumers to swallow.

Is it harmful

Clara rates this NEUTRAL, a limited-concern additive. This rating is based on its synthetic origin and the fact that it has a numeric acceptable daily intake. EFSA established a daily limit of 24 mg per kg of body weight for this additive. Because it is a large polymer, it is not absorbed by the digestive system and passes through the body unchanged, meaning it does not accumulate in tissues or enter the bloodstream.

What the science says

Both European and American regulators have evaluated the safety of this polymer. EFSA conducted a thorough re-evaluation of the additive and concluded that it does not raise safety concerns at the levels currently used in food supplements. The evaluation confirmed that the substance is non-genotoxic and is excreted from the body without being metabolised. No adverse effects were observed in animal studies even at high doses, which allowed regulators to establish a secure daily intake limit.

Where you'll find it

You will find this ingredient almost exclusively in solid food supplements, such as vitamin tablets, mineral capsules, and herbal formulations. On ingredient lists, it is typically declared by its full name, polyvinylpyrrolidone-vinyl acetate copolymer, or by its European code, E1208. On US labels, it is listed as polyvinylpyrrolidone-vinyl acetate copolymer under federal regulations.
