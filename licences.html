Recipes and recipe photos

MyPlate Kitchen — recipes and photos from MyPlate Kitchen, U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA). Public domain: works of the U.S. Government are not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105).

MyPlate Kitchen's recipe pages were retired from myplate.gov in 2026; Clara read them, once, from the copies kept by the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine. The recipe photos Clara shows are served from this site (clarascan.app/recipes/img/), made from those copies at most 640 pixels wide. Clara adapts the recipes — titles in sentence case, each ingredient matched to a food table to give its nutrition, a recipe's own nutrition checked against USDA's analysis — and names the source on every recipe as “USDA MyPlate Kitchen (archived)”. USDA does not endorse Clara.

Clara's own recipes — written for Clara by Scandihouse Limited.
